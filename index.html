The apps

Jay-C

Calls and streams

Voice and video calls, game and screen streaming up to 1080p60, and servers with rooms and text channels, on a server you or a friend runs in Docker.

  • Clear voice that doesn't muffle your other apps.
  • Stream a game, a window or the whole screen, with its sound.
  • Up to 16 people per room. No accounts, no ads.
Download GitHub

Jay-A

Audio

Filters for your microphone, which Jay-C can use in its calls, plus a soundboard with global hotkeys, an audio editor and a batch converter.

  • AI noise removal takes out keyboards, clicks and fans.
  • Your own chain of filter, gate, EQ, compressor, de-esser and limiter.
  • Lives in the tray and only opens the microphone while it's in use.
Download GitHub

Jay-E

Documents and media

Read, edit and convert documents, images and videos without an office suite.

  • Word, OpenDocument and PDF edited in place.
  • Markdown and LaTeX with a live preview, math included.
  • Convert photos and videos in batches; cut and join video without re-encoding.
Download GitHub

Jay-S

Study

Exams, mock tests and flashcards made from your own material by Jay-AI, a model that runs on your computer.

  • Bring PDFs, Word files, text or photos of your notes.
  • Exam styles such as ENEM, UEPG and UEM, with answer keys and analysed results.
  • Nothing leaves your PC.
Download GitHub

Jay-H

The hub

One app to install, update and open every Jay app, and it updates itself the same way.

  • The installed version and the latest one on GitHub, side by side.
  • Installers only run if their SHA-256 matches GitHub's.
  • "Update all" with one click.
